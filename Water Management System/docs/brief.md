
Project Brief: Community Water Service Concern Recording and Tracking System

1. The problem
Residents have difficulty reporting water service concerns and receiving updates about the status of their reports. Meanwhile, water service administrators and managers struggle to organize resident information, manage reported concerns, assign personnel, and monitor service activities.

2. Users and what they need to do
User role	What they need to do in the app
Resident 	Register and log in to their account, submit water service concerns, and track report status.
Admin/ Manager 	Log in, manage resident records, review concerns, and assign personnel.
Personnel	Log in, view assigned concerns, and record actions taken.


3. The database behind it
Database name: WaterServiceDB
Number of tables: 8
Table	Description
USER_ACCOUNT	Stores user login credentials and account information for accessing the system.
RESIDENT	Stores residents’ personal and contact information
SERVICE_CONCERN	Records water service problems submitted by residents
PERSONNEL	Stores information about water service employees
ASSIGNMENT	Manages the assignment of personnel to reported concerns
SERVICE_ACTION	Records actions and solutions taken to address concerns
WATER_SCHEDULE	Stores water distribution schedules and service information
NOTIFICATION	Stores announcements and notifications for residents or users


4. Must-have features (for the final project)
1. Register and log in to resident accounts securely.
2. Add resident records and manage their information.
3. Submit water service concerns with descriptions and concern types.
4. View submitted concerns and track their current status.
5. Assign personnel to reported water service concerns.
6. Record service actions and solutions performed by personnel.
7. Manage water distribution schedules and send notifications.


5. Nice-to-have features (only if there's time)
1. Add a dashboard showing the total number of reported, pending, and resolved concerns.
2. Add a search and filter function for faster record management.
3. Send automatic notifications when a concern's status changes.



6. Similar apps I looked at
1. PrimeWater
Idea to borrow: Concern submission and ticket tracking.
Application: Allow residents to submit water service complaints and monitor their status.

2. Maynilad
Idea to borrow: Organizing customer concerns and providing updates through different communications channels.
