Community Water Service Concern Recording and Tracking System

Project Description

The Community Water Service Concern Recording and Tracking System is designed to help residents report and track water service concerns. It also allows authorized personnel to manage reports, assignments, service actions, water distribution schedules, and notifications.

Project Information

Industry: Water Utility and Public Service Management

Location: Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Database Tables

1. RESIDENT
2. SERVICE_CONCERN
3. USER_ACCOUNT
4. PERSONNEL
5. ASSIGNMENT
6. SERVICE_ACTION
7. WATER_SCHEDULE
8. NOTIFICATION

Project Purpose

To provide an organized system for recording, monitoring, and managing community water service concerns.
